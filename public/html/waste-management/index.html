About Us

» About Us

Who We Are

BlueField Group is one of the fastest growing agricultural companies, with over two decades of experience, operating in the dynamic and diverse MENA region. We provide innovative agricultural products and services, as well as exceptional services for the public health, landscaping, and cleaning sectors for UAE, Jordan, Lebanon, and Iraq.

Founded in 2000, BlueField Group has grown into a distinguished entity with an extensive portfolio of services, products, and solutions, serving several countries in the MENA.

Purpose

At BlueField Group, our purpose is to innovate and transform the agriculture industry first as well as other sectors we serve in the MENA. We are dedicated to expanding our diverse business portfolio, positively impacting countries across the region, and contributing to the region’s growth, sustainability, and prosperity.

Mission

We strive to deliver state-of-the-art agricultural products and services that not only meet but exceed the evolving needs of our region. Leveraging our decades of expertise, we are committed to delivering cutting-edge agricultural solutions, supporting farmers, empowering our communities, and fostering economic growth.

Vision

Our vision is to be the leading catalyst for positive change in the MENA for the agricultural sector and other sectors we serve. We aspire to be recognized as the partner for innovative, sustainable, and technology-driven products and services. We envision a MENA region where agriculture is booming, communities are thriving, and our company stands as a symbol of progress and excellence in the region.

Values

Innovation

We are dedicated to bringing the latest innovation and advancements with the help of our engineers, researchers, and business developers to provide clients with the most innovative services and practices.

Sustainability

We aim to create positive environmental and social impact everywhere we operate, from supporting farmers, women empowerment initiatives, to partnerships for sustainable growth, and more.

Quality and Reliability

We place a strong focus on delivering high quality products and services to our customers, helping them rely on our offerings and solutions.

Customer-Centric

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. We are dedicated to understanding their needs, providing exceptional service, and delivering products that exceed their expectations.

Integrity

We conduct our business with the highest level of integrity and ethics. We are transparent, honest, and trustworthy in all our interactions with customers, partners, and employees.

Timeline

1998

Our founder visited Iraq for the first time with the aim of defining our next steps.

2000

BlueField was founded to address the Iraqi market needs. Starting in a small Beirut office, our focus was initially on transforming vegetable seeds to enhance crop yields in Iraq and to export 40 metric tons of agricultural plastic rolls to meet the market demands.

2003

BlueField started with potato tubers and greenhouse covers, delivering approximately 60 metric tons to the Iraqi market.

2005

BlueField’s portfolio expanded its vegetables products to cover tomato and cucumber. We consistently seek innovative enhancements to introduce into the market.

2006

BlueField Agriculture Lebanon was established to meet the agricultural demands of the Lebanese market by providing high-quality seeds, fertilizers, and introducing potato tubers.

2007

BlueField Agriculture Iraq, was founded in Erbil. It established a distribution network and gained dominance in the Iraqi tomato market. It introduced a new tomato variety called Oula, which has become a common and recognizable variety.

2008

BlueField Agriculture Iraq expanded its operations to Baghdad.

2009

BlueField Agriculture Iraq took a significant step due to the potential demand in the Iraqi market to cater the growing needs.

2011

BlueField Public Health was founded with the aim of serving the pest management and food safety sectors through carefully choosing applied solutions and high-quality workforce.

2012

BlueField Group established Albizia Landscape Services to provide personalized landscape services and BF Core Cleaning Services Iraq to provide cleaning services.

2020

BlueField Agriculture UAE and BlueField Agriculture Jordan were established to serve neighboring markets.

2021

BlueField Agriculture Iraq introduced field crop seeds in Iraq.
